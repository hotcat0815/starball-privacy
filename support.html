EnglishiOS 隐私政策

联系我们

如果游戏无法启动、关卡或存档异常，或看完视频后没有获得复活奖励，请通过邮件联系我们。

yoyodreamgame@126.com

邮件中可附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、游戏版本、发生问题的关卡和操作步骤。截图请先遮住个人信息。请勿发送密码、身份证号码或证件照片。

遇到问题时

  • 游戏卡住或闪退时，可先完全退出再打开，并确认是否有可用更新。
  • 复活视频未显示时，可检查网络连接，稍后再试或重新挑战。如果完整观看后仍未复活，请告诉我们发生时间和关卡。
  • 游戏进度保存在设备上。排查问题时请先保留 App，删除 App 会清除本地进度。

iOS Support

For launch problems, level or save issues, missing revive rewards or other questions about Pixel Pinball: Star Island Journey, email yoyodreamgame@126.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, game version and the steps that led to the issue. Remove personal information from screenshots. Please do not send passwords or identity documents.

If the game freezes, try closing it completely and reopening it. If a rewarded ad is unavailable, check your connection and try again later or retry the level. Your progress is stored locally, so keep the app installed while troubleshooting.
