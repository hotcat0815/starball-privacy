存档和防沉迷

游戏进度、成绩、设置和隐私同意记录保存在设备上。游戏不提供账号注册或云存档。删除 App 可移除其本地数据，也会清除游戏进度。

若当前版本和游戏语言启用了本地防沉迷，游戏会在设备上校验您填写的姓名及身份证号码的格式，并根据证件日期计算年龄分组。游戏仅在本地保存分组结果，不保存或上传您填写的姓名及证件号码，也不会把它们交给广告或统计 SDK。此功能不核实身份的真实性。

游戏启动时会从 GitHub Pages 读取公开的功能开关文件。请求不包含姓名、证件号码、年龄分组或游戏存档。网站服务在响应连接时会接收 IP 地址等连接信息。GitHub 隐私声明

iOS Privacy Policy

Effective and updated November 11, 2026. This policy covers the iOS edition of Pixel Pinball: Star Island Journey, operated by Beijing Youyou Chuangxiang Technology Co., Ltd.

Local saves and play limits

The game stores progress, scores, settings and your privacy choice on your device. It has no account registration or cloud saves. Deleting the app removes its local data, including your progress.

If the local play-limit feature is enabled for your game version and language, the app checks the format of the name and ID number you enter and uses the date in the number to calculate an age group on your device. Only the age group is saved locally. Your name and ID number are neither stored nor uploaded or shared with the SDKs. This check does not verify your identity.

At startup the app reads a public settings file from GitHub Pages. The request does not contain the form data, age group or save data. The site receives connection information such as your IP address to serve the request. GitHub Privacy Statement

Advertising and analytics

The following services start after you agree to the in-game privacy notice.

GroMore / Pangle

Beijing Ocean Engine Network Technology Co., Ltd. and its affiliates provide banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads, ad measurement, attribution and fraud prevention. The SDK may process device and system information, available device identifiers, app information, IP address, network status, ad views and clicks, and performance or error data. The app configures restrictions on personalized ads and supported data access. Processing depends on the SDK version, settings and device permissions.

GroMore privacy policy · Pangle SDK privacy policy

Umeng Analytics (U-App)

Umeng Tongxin (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd. helps measure usage, retention, play time and gameplay. The SDK may process device and app versions, available device identifiers such as IDFV, IP address, network status, launch and usage records. The game sends summary events such as level, play time, scores, failures, revives and tutorial steps. These events do not include names, ID numbers or complete save files. The app does not enable Umeng’s Apple Search Ads or SKAdNetwork attribution options.
