1. Operating entities and scope of application

"Pixel Pinball: Star Island Journey" (hereinafter referred to as "this game") is developed byBeijing Youyou Chuangxiang Technology Co., Ltd.(hereinafter referred to as "we") operates. This policy applies to the Android application of this game and this privacy policy display page. The current Android package name iscom.yycx.starball。

Please read this policy before use, especially the third-party advertising services and minors protection sections. Third-party platforms, external web pages and applications you install separately shall have their own privacy policies applicable to their independently provided services. This policy does not replace third-party policies, nor does it mean that we can exempt ourselves from our personal information protection responsibilities.

2. How we process information

1. Local game data

In order to save your playing experience, the game saves level unlocking, clearance records, scores, star ratings, challenge records, appearance selection, guidance completion status, sound and animation settings, privacy policy authorized version and other data in the application private storage space. The current version does not have its own account or cloud archive server; after you agree to the privacy policy, the main level events described below will be reported through Umeng, and the complete archive will not be uploaded.

2. Advertising services

This version aggregates Pangolin and Tencent advertising through GroMore, providing banners, voluntary viewing incentive videos and inter-level screens. Tencent Advertising SDK participates in ad loading and display according to schedule after user consent, and processes OAID, device and system information, application and network information, ad interaction and error data. The specific scope and optional controls are subject to the official privacy policy and SDK configuration. When the advertising service provider changes, the confirmation will be prompted again, and the advertisement will not be initialized before confirmation.

After you select "Agree and enter the game", the game initializes the advertising SDK for bottom banner display and resurrection of the incentive video you actively selected. Pangolin/GroMore SDK will directly collect and process device identification Android ID through its code, as well as device brand and model, system and API version, language, time zone, screen parameters, CPU and storage status, IP address, network type and status, operator, application package name and version, front and back status, ad display/click/conversion and errors, performance, anti-cheating related information for ad loading, performance statistics, attribution, security and service stability. The specific processing scope of the SDK is affected by version, device and privacy configuration. See Section 3 for details. Closing the banner only hides the banner of the current page, and will re-request display after switching to other game pages; this does not mean withdrawing all advertising service authorization.

The current advertising integration limits personalized recommendations through the SDK privacy control interface, and limits controllable collection capabilities such as positioning, phone status, application list, OAID, Wi-Fi information, and external storage writing. Android ID is not a collection item that has been completely turned off: the advertising SDK will process Android ID after your consent for ad display, performance statistics and attribution, and anti-cheating; its privacy interface is used to limit the frequency of collection, which does not mean that reading is completely prohibited. The game business code does not read the Android ID itself, nor does it actively provide a custom device identifier to the advertising SDK.These restrictions do not mean that advertising does not require an Internet connection or does not process device information at all. If the creative uses sensor interaction, the SDK may process sensor information such as acceleration, gyroscope, etc. according to its version and device capabilities.

3. Test diagnosis

The test version that enables ad debugging logs will record events such as ad loading, closing, reward verification, error codes, and SDK versions locally on the device for locating anomalies; the log file will be re-recorded when it reaches approximately 256 KB. The application does not automatically send this local log to us. If you take the initiative to report a problem, you can choose to provide necessary screenshots, device information or logs. Please cover up irrelevant personal information first.

4. Contact us

When you consult us or make a request for personal information, we will process the contact information, request content and necessary materials you actively provide to reply, verify the request and handle the problem. Please do not send passwords, photos of full ID, or sensitive information that is not relevant to the question; when verification is required, we will explain the reason and minimize the information required.

5. Umeng Statistics

When it is started for the first time and the privacy policy has not been agreed, neither the advertising nor the statistical services are initialized. After you click "Agree and enter the game", advertising and Umeng Statistics will be opened together, and the Umeng Mobile Statistics SDK will be initialized to analyze new and active users, retention, usage time, as well as the gameplay and guidance of the main line, boss, daily, and endless modes. The processing scope includes device identification information (Android ID, OAID, GUID, GAID, the actual situation depends on the device and SDK capabilities), device model and system version, application version, IP Address and network status, start-up and front-end and back-end usage records; gameplay events include mode, level or wave, boss difficulty, play seconds, score, scene, remaining bricks, number of failures, exit or retry options, resurrection process results, ball group peak, first avatar and the time taken to form a ball group, avatar chain, number of props caught and missed, and guidance steps completed or skipped. The data is used to discover problems with difficulty, rhythm, props and resurrection experience. It does not come with names, phone numbers, emails, precise touch tracks or complete archives; the game is aggregated locally by game and is not uploaded frame by frame. When statistics are not enabled, these custom events will not be cached or reported, and automatic testing and each mode test entrance will not be included in the gameplay analysis. Starting from 0.44.7, the ordinary version upgrade will use the existing consent record. Not only will pop-ups appear repeatedly due to version updates, but the prompt will be displayed again after the consent has not been agreed, has been withdrawn, or the application data has been cleared. The current version does not provide a separate statistics switch. After agreeing to save the record, the corresponding service will be automatically opened upon subsequent startup; video resurrection still requires your active selection and will not play automatically.

Umeng Mobile Statistics uses common 10.0.1 and asms 1.8.7.2 basic components. It does not access push, sharing, uninstall analysis or advanced operational analysis components; it closes the application list, Wi-Fi MAC and installation source collection interface, and does not apply for positioning, phone status or external storage permissions. The advertising SDK's restriction on identifiers is only for advertising services and does not mean that Umeng Statistics does not process device identifiers.

4. Device permission description

  • Network access and network status:is used for ad loading, statistical reporting, network judgment and opening policy web pages after you choose to enable it.
  • Wi-Fi network status:is used for Umeng statistics to determine network and reconnection after being enabled; the Wi-Fi MAC collection interface has been closed.
  • vibration:is used for game interaction feedback when this function is supported, and is not used for reading personal information.
  • Storage:Game archives and test logs are written to the app's private space without reading your photo album or other app files.

The basic gameplay of this game does not require you to grant read and write permissions to the camera, microphone, address book, precise location, phone status, or external storage. For system capabilities and permission descriptions of advertising services, please refer to the third-party policies at the same time. You can view and manage the actual permissions listed on your device through the Android system "Settings → Application → This Game → Permissions".

5. Saving, deletion and security

Game progress and settings are saved in the device's app private space, usually until you clear the app data or uninstall it. Clearing data will delete the local archive and the current version cannot be restored via the cloud. The information processed by the Advertising and Statistics SDK is managed by its provider in accordance with the storage location, period and security measures of the corresponding policy. You can learn more through the link on this page or contact its provider to exercise your rights.

The consultation materials you send us will only be retained for the period required to process the request and are required by law, and will be deleted or anonymized after the purpose is achieved. We adopt necessary access control, minimization of processing and other measures to protect information; no system can guarantee absolute security. If an information security incident affects your rights and interests, we will take remedial and notification measures in accordance with the law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Reject or withdraw:If you select "disagree and exit" in the privacy prompt that requires confirmation when starting the game for the first time, the game will exit without initializing advertising and statistics. After agreeing, you can read the policy again in "Advertising and Privacy" in the homepage settings, select "Disagree and Exit" to withdraw authorization, stop subsequent ad requests and game statistics events, call the statistics SDK stop interface and exit the game. Re-display the prompt next time you start it. Withdrawal will not automatically delete information that has been processed before; if it involves information held by a third party, you can make a request through the channels listed in its policy, and we will assist in processing it.
  • Inquiry, correction, copy or delete:You may contact us to make a request for personal information. We will verify the necessary information and process it in accordance with the law, or explain the reasons why it cannot be processed; when it comes to information held independently by the advertising service party, we will provide its contact path and assist you in making a request.
  • Local data deletion:You can clear the game application data or uninstall it in the Android system settings. Please confirm whether you need to retain local game progress before operating.
  • account cancellation:The current version does not have the account registration function for this game, so there is no corresponding account cancellation process. Accounts used through third-party platforms should be processed on that platform.

If you have any objection to the processing results, you can complain to the relevant competent authorities and seek relief in accordance with the law.
