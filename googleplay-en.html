Google Play Edition Privacy Policy

Pixel Pinball: Star Island Journey

Update date: October 28, 2026

1. Scope of application

This policy applies to the Google Play channel version with the package name com.yycx.starball, and the operating entity is Beijing Youyou Chuangxiang Technology Co., Ltd. This channel uses Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics and does not integrate Umeng, GroMore, Pangolin or Tencent Advertising SDK.

is currently a beta advertising version. When the Firebase project has not been configured, the statistics service will not be initialized and data will not be reported. The configuration of actual projects, advertising spaces, and developer contact channels must be completed before official release.

2. Local game data

Level progress, stars, score records, appearance, language and privacy selections are saved locally on the device, and no account registration is required. Clearing app data or uninstalling may delete local archives.

3. Google advertising service

Use AdMob to display banners, rewarded videos and interstitial screens after consent. The incentive video is actively selected by the player, and resurrection will be granted only after receiving a valid reward callback. Interstitial screens follow the in-game frequency limit.

Google Advertising SDK may process device and application information, advertising identifiers, IP and network information, ad interaction and diagnostic data based on service configuration for delivery, measurement and anti-cheating. Please refer to Google’s official disclosure for specific types and processing methods.

Google UMP collects and updates advertising privacy choices where applicable; actual ad requests are controlled by their permission status. Google Ads Privacy Options in Settings can be used to review your choices.

Google AdMob Data Disclosure

4. Statistical analysis

After configuring a Firebase project and obtaining consent, Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics 4) analyzes launch, activity, and game events, including mode, level, completion or failure, play time, highest number of balls, combos, items, and resurrection processes. The game does not actively send names, email addresses or phone numbers.

This implementation rejects by default the use of statistics for ad storage, ad user data, or personalized ads; ad privacy choices are managed independently by UMP. Game events to be uploaded will not be cached without consent, and subsequent statistics will be stopped after the consent is withdrawn.

Firebase Privacy and Security Instructions

5. Your choices and rights

You can view the policy, withdraw your consent, or turn the Google Ads Privacy Options back on in the game settings. If you do not agree to the first service authorization, you will exit the game. Withdrawal will not delete data that has been previously processed by the service party in accordance with the law; if you need to make an inquiry or deletion request, please contact us through the developer contact channel displayed on the Google Play Store.

Google services may process data outside of your country or region. The retention period and cross-border protection measures are subject to its policies and actual configuration. This game does not save advertising logos in the archive.

Google Privacy Policy

6. Minors and policy updates

If you are a minor, please use it under the guidance of your guardian. Game audience, content rating and advertising settings should be subject to official disclosure by the store. When services or processing methods change, this policy will be updated simultaneously and consent will be sought again when necessary.
